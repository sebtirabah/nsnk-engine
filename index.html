Executive Overview

The NSNK Engine introduces a structurally grounded alignment architecture based on Normative Structural Neo-Kantianism (NSNK). Unlike post-hoc filtering methods (such as RLHF or Constitutional AI), NSNK achieves Normative Internalization (NI) by embedding non-compensatory moral constraints directly into judgment formation (J = F(S, N, X)).

Key Architectural Features

1. The Maxim Unit: M = \langle x, c, g \rangle

Re-represents candidate actions by explicitly coupling the proposed action (x), context/circumstances (c), and operative end (g).

2. Simultaneous Non-Compensatory Pipeline

Enforces the Humanity Floor Constraint (H(M) \ge H_{\min}), Universalization (G(M)), and Internal Consistency (IC(M)) simultaneously before candidate decisions enter execution.

3. Constitutive Test ($\Delta N \implies \Delta J$)

Verifies that changes in normative structure systematically alter judgment formation itself, distinguishing internal functional access from post-hoc external filtering.
